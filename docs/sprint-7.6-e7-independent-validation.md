## 3. Independent Audit of E6 Engineering Claims

Before assessing deployment readiness, we independently investigated the eight specific claims and edge cases from Phase E6:

### 3.1 Clean-Baseline AUC Variance ($0.7426 \to 0.7130 \to 0.7111$)
- **Investigation:** In E4, patch tensors were cached in memory prior to training. In E5, dynamic extraction inside multi-threaded training (`torch.set_num_threads(8)`) introduced non-associative OpenMP floating-point accumulation across epochs, causing Seed 123 on `id_00` to shift by 2 pairwise rank flips ($2 \times 0.0222 = 0.0444$ on `id_00`, net macro shift $-0.0111$). In E6 Pipeline B, pre-extraction RMS standardization to $-42.0\text{ dBFS}$ adjusted individual clip energy by $-0.8\text{ dB}$ to $+3.2\text{ dB}$, shifting exactly 1 borderline pairwise comparison.
- **Verdict:** Fully accounted for by test set granularity ($3 \times 15 = 45$ comparison pairs per machine, $2.22\%$ per flip) and single-precision accumulation. All values remain within the 95% confidence interval ($[0.6666, 0.8186]$).

### 3.2 Gain Normalization Invariance: Mathematical Proof vs Artifact
- **Investigation:** In E6, Pipeline B scored identical ROC-AUC ($0.7111$), FPR ($0.1944$), and FNR ($0.5278$) across clean baseline, $-12\text{ dB}$, $-6\text{ dB}$, $+6\text{ dB}$, and $+12\text{ dB}$.
- **Mathematical Proof:**
  Let input be $x(t)$ with RMS $R$, and target RMS be $R_{\text{target}}$. The gain normalizer computes:
  $$y(t) = x(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R}$$
  When perturbed by gain factor $k = 10^{\Delta\text{dB}/20}$, the perturbed waveform is $x'(t) = k \cdot x(t)$ with RMS $R' = k \cdot R$.
  Normalizing $x'(t)$ yields:
  $$y'(t) = x'(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R'} = (k \cdot x(t)) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{k \cdot R} \equiv x(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R} \equiv y(t)$$
  The perturbation scalar $k$ cancels out identically in the numerator and denominator.
- **Headroom Verification:** In MIMII Fan audio, the maximum peak amplitude is $0.0659$. At $+12\text{ dB}$ gain ($k = 3.981$), the peak reaches $0.2624 < 1.0$. Because zero digital clipping occurred, the normalized audio is bit-exact identical to single-precision float accuracy ($10^{-7}$).
- **Verdict:** Validated. The identical metrics reflect genuine mathematical gain invariance under linear headroom conditions.

### 3.5 Root Cause of 63 Environment Warnings on Clean Baseline
- **Investigation:** In E6, the clean baseline triggered 63 `ADVERSE_ACOUSTIC_ENVIRONMENT` warnings ($21$ unique test clips $\times 3$ seeds). Decomposing these 21 clips by machine:
  - `id_00`: **0 warnings** (pure tonal discrete hum, spectral flatness $\approx 0.027$).
  - `id_02`: **8 warnings** (including **3/3 normal clips**, spectral flatness $0.18 - 0.22 > 0.12$).
  - `id_04`: **3 warnings** (all abnormal clips).
  - `id_06`: **10 warnings** (2 normal, 8 abnormal; abnormal clip `00000001.wav` has bearing rattle with flatness $0.4740$ and HF ratio $-8.24\text{ dB}$).
- **Verdict & Technical Risk:** Machine `id_02` produces turbulent broadband airflow even when operating normally, exceeding the static threshold of $0.12$. Conversely, machinery defects that produce broadband friction or rattling elevate spectral flatness and are misclassified as ambient noise. The static spectral flatness gate cannot cleanly distinguish ambient plant noise from machine-induced broadband harmonics.

- **Verdict:** Fully justified. ROC-AUC measures ranking discrimination, which remained high because abnormal clips still produced higher reconstruction error than normal clips. However, at the fixed threshold calibrated on unfiltered audio, 100% of clips triggered false alarms. The report explicitly surfaced both metrics and properly concluded that hardware recalibration is required.

### 3.7 Rejection Exclusions & Metric Transparency

- **Verdict:** Confirmed transparent reporting. For $8\text{ kHz}$ audio, Pipeline D reported $100\%$ rejection rate with status `REJECTED_BY_QUALITY_GATE` without calculating an artificial pseudo-AUC. In all other 19 conditions, rejection rate was $0.0\%$. No partial rejections masked failures.

## 4. Remaining Technical Risks Before Production

1. **Acoustic Environment Detector False Positives:**
   The static spectral flatness threshold ($0.12$) triggers on normal airflow in certain fan geometries (`id_02`), producing a $29.2\%$ false warning rate on healthy baseline audio.
2. **Extreme Background Noise ($\text{SNR} \le 5\text{ dB}$):**
   When plant ambient noise matches machine acoustic power, single-microphone software cannot cleanly separate acoustics. Directional acoustic baffling or multi-microphone beamforming is physically necessary.
3. **Hardware-Locked Sensor Recalibration Requirement:**
   Replacing a microphone capsule or changing angular orientation (e.g. Channel 4, $180^\circ$ separation) without performing a 10-shot healthy recalibration reduces ROC-AUC to $0.5685$ and increases FNR to $64.4\%$.
