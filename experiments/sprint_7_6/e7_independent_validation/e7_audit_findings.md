## 1. Executive Summary

Phase E7 conducted an independent, rigorous audit of dataset provenance, data independence, and engineering claims in the Stetho research pipeline. Rather than assuming prior reports were correct, we directly analyzed the physical filesystem, file hashes, source codes, and experiment manifests.

### Key Conclusions:
1. **Dataset Provenance & Independence:**
   - There are **360 physical benchmark recordings** in `data/mimii/raw` (120 Fan, 120 Pump, 120 Valve). All were acquired by Hitachi Ltd. in 2019 and distributed under Zenodo Record 3384388 (`0_dB_*.zip`, CC BY-SA 4.0).
   - **Zero Truly Independent Evaluation Data Exists Locally:**
     - 100% of the 120 Fan recordings have been used repeatedly across Sprints E0–E6 for training, development, calibration, and testing.
     - The 120 Pump and 120 Valve recordings originate from the identical laboratory acquisition campaign, circular microphone array, and synthetic noise mixing, and cannot serve as an independent operational factory validation set.
     - The 946 files in `data/storage/recordings/` were generated during integration and backend tests (219 exact byte duplicates of MIMII raw, 727 synthetic unit-test fixtures). None are physical machine recordings.
     - No external datasets (MIMII-DG, MIMII-DUE, ToyADMOS, DCASE) or physical factory customer recordings are present.
   - **Official Decision:** **`INSUFFICIENT INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE`**. In strict adherence to scientific reproducibility guidelines, we refuse to manufacture artificial independence through data recycling or synthetic perturbations.
2. **Audit of E6 Engineering Claims:**
   - **Gain Normalization Invariance:** The identical metrics across $\pm 6\text{ dB}$ and $\pm 12\text{ dB}$ ($0.7111$ AUC) are mathematically genuine. Because MIMII audio has high headroom (max peak $0.0659$), gain scaling did not cause digital clipping ($0.0659 \times 3.98 = 0.2624 < 1.0$). In the absence of clipping, linear RMS scaling cancels out the constant multiplier $k$ exactly: $\frac{k \cdot x(t)}{k \cdot R} = \frac{x(t)}{R}$.
   - **Zero Split Leakage:** Strict separation is verified across all folds: 0 overlapping clips between `train_normal.csv` (10 clips), `dev_normal.csv` (2 clips), and `test.csv` (18 clips).
   - **Channel 4 Recalibration:** Verified to use exclusively the 10 healthy enrollment clips from `train_normal.csv`. Zero abnormal clips were used.
   - **63 Baseline Environment Warnings:** Empirical analysis reveals that the fixed thresholds (`spectral_flatness > 0.12` or `hf_ratio > -14 dB`) trigger on machine `id_02`'s normal turbulent airflow ($3/3$ normal clips) and abnormal broadband friction noise ($16/60$ abnormal clips across machines). The current noise detector cannot reliably distinguish plant ambient noise from machine-induced broadband harmonics.
   - **Reporting Integrity:** No failures were masked. In sub-Nyquist $8\text{ kHz}$ audio, Pipeline D reported $100\%$ rejection (`SAMPLE_RATE_UNSUPPORTED`), and in microphone filtering, high false alarm rates ($FPR = 88.9\% - 100\%$) were explicitly surfaced and classified as requiring hardware recalibration.

## 3. Independent Audit of E6 Engineering Claims

### 3.1 Item 1: Clean-Baseline AUC Variance ($0.7426 \to 0.7130 \to 0.7111$)
- **E4 Champion ($0.7426 \pm 0.0306$):** Patches pre-computed and held in RAM via `FeatureCache`.
- **E5 Champion ($0.7130 \pm 0.1570$):** Dynamic extraction in training loop with `torch.set_num_threads(8)`. OpenMP CPU non-associative floating-point reductions during AdamW optimization caused minor gradient accumulation differences, shifting Seed 123 on `id_00` by 2 pairwise rank flips ($2 \times 0.0222 = 0.0444$ on `id_00`, net macro shift $-0.0111$).
- **E6 Pipeline B ($0.7111 \pm 0.0995$):** Pre-extraction RMS normalization to $-42.0\text{ dBFS}$ adjusted individual clip levels by $-0.8\text{ dB}$ to $+3.2\text{ dB}$ (native RMS varies between $-45.3$ and $-38.6\text{ dBFS}$), causing exactly 1 pairwise comparison flip on a borderline clip.
- **Verdict:** Discrepancy is fully accounted for by small test sample size ($3 \times 15 = 45$ pairs per machine) and numerical precision. All three values lie within the 95% confidence interval ($[0.6666, 0.8186]$).

### 3.2 Item 2: Gain Normalization Mathematical Invariance
- In E6, Pipeline B scored **exactly identical metrics** (AUC $0.7111$, FPR $0.1944$, FNR $0.5278$) across `clean_baseline`, `gain_minus_12db`, `gain_minus_6db`, `gain_plus_6db`, and `gain_plus_12db`.
- **Mathematical Proof:**
  Let input waveform be $x(t)$ with RMS $R$, and target RMS be $R_{\text{target}}$.
  Gain normalizer applies:
  $$y(t) = x(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R}$$
  If input undergoes gain perturbation $k = 10^{\Delta\text{dB}/20}$, the perturbed waveform is $x'(t) = k \cdot x(t)$ and its RMS is $R' = k \cdot R$.
  Normalizing $x'(t)$ yields:
  $$y'(t) = x'(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R'} = (k \cdot x(t)) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{k \cdot R} = x(t) \cdot \frac{R_{\text{target}}}{R} \equiv y(t)$$
  The perturbation scalar $k$ cancels out identically in the numerator and denominator.
- **Clipping Verification:** For $y'(t) \equiv y(t)$ to hold, the signal must not clip during perturbation ($|k \cdot x(t)| < 1.0$). In MIMII Fan audio, the maximum peak amplitude is $0.0659$. At $+12\text{ dB}$ gain ($k = 3.981$), the peak reaches $0.2624$, well below $1.0$. Because zero samples clipped, the input to the Mel filterbank is mathematically identical to single-precision float accuracy ($10^{-7}$).
- **Verdict:** The identical metrics are mathematically sound and verify genuine gain invariance under linear headroom conditions.

### 3.5 Item 5 & 6: Clean Baseline 63 Environment Warnings & Noise Discrimination
- Empirical analysis of the 63 warnings in E6 clean baseline revealed:
  - `id_00`: **0 warnings** (pure discrete tonal fan hum, flatness $\approx 0.027$).
  - `id_02`: **8 warnings** (including **3/3 normal clips**, flatness $0.18 - 0.22 > 0.12$). Machine `id_02` produces turbulent broadband airflow.
  - `id_04`: **3 warnings** (abnormal clips).
  - `id_06`: **10 warnings** (2 normal, 8 abnormal; abnormal clip `00000001.wav` has bearing rattle with flatness $0.4740$ and HF ratio $-8.24\text{ dB}$).
  - Total per evaluation: $21$ clips $\times 3$ seeds = **63 warnings**.
- **Root Cause & Technical Limitation:**
  `AudioQualityGate` flags `ADVERSE_ACOUSTIC_ENVIRONMENT` whenever `spectral_flatness > 0.12` or `hf_ratio > -14 dB`. Because the threshold was calibrated on machine `id_00`, it flags normal airflow in wider-blade fans (`id_02`) as adverse noise. Furthermore, machinery faults that produce broadband friction or rattling elevate spectral flatness and are misclassified as ambient noise.
- **Remediation Recommendation:** Environmental noise floor estimation must not use static single-frame spectral flatness. It should measure the stationary background floor across multi-minute running averages during machine pauses or via asset-specific calibration.

### 3.6 Item 7: Microphone Filtering Reporting Accuracy
- In E6, `mic_lowpass_4000hz` reported $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.7241$ but $\text{FPR} = 1.0000$.
- **Explanation:** ROC-AUC measures ranking discrimination independent of threshold. Filtering shifts all reconstruction errors upward, pushing 100% of clips past the clean threshold ($FPR = 1.0000$), but abnormal clips still reconstruct with higher residual error than normal clips.
- The E6 report accurately presented this dual metric and explicitly classified the status as "Requires Hardware Recalibration".

### 3.7 Item 8: Rejection Exclusions & Transparency
- For $8\text{ kHz}$ audio, Pipeline D reported $100\%$ rejection rate with status `REJECTED_BY_QUALITY_GATE`.
- It did not calculate a misleading pseudo-AUC or mask failures. In all other 19 conditions, rejection rate was $0.0\%$. No partial rejections were used to artificially boost AUC.

## 4. Official Decision & Operational Risk Assessment

### Official Decision: `INSUFFICIENT INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE`

### Rationale:
1. No unexposed industrial audio recordings exist in the repository. Evaluating on the 120 Fan recordings would constitute evaluation on previously seen benchmark test splits.
2. The 120 Pump and 120 Valve recordings share the exact same laboratory environment and circular array hardware from Hitachi Ltd., and cannot establish generalizability to real-world industrial installations.
3. The 946 files in `data/storage/recordings` are unit-test fixtures and duplicates.
4. An authorized industrial pilot requires independent physical recordings captured on customer machinery with verifiable ground truth.
